# GALA facts changelog

## 2026-10-10 — first fact check

**Changed**
- `kennon.status`: "open up to 5 tons, one-lane rockshed, Acrow bridge 2.5 m clearance" → still light vehicles only (5 t), but the **Camp 2 Acrow Bridge is now closed** (one-lane detour), plus two one-lane sections at Camp 6. It was closed to non-residents Aug 3–22 during heavy rain. (DPWH-CAR advisory Oct 9, via Baguio City Guide)
- `tolls.segments.class1`: the old figures (SCTEX ₱311, TPLEX ₱370) didn't match any source I could open → now **NLEX+SCTEX to Tarlac ₱670, TPLEX to Rosario ₱311**, NLEX to Sta. Ines ₱440. (Expressway.ph, GMA)
- `rfid`: added One RFID (optional merged Easytrip + Autosweep account, launched Oct 2025). Dropped the "barrier delay and citation" line because no source for it opened.
- `baguio.visitors`: now only the 2025 DOT-Cordillera figure (1,586,890; 72.92% Baguio). Dropped the 2023 and 2022 numbers.

**Confirmed**
- `tolls.manila-baguio` ₱981 / ₱2,337 / ₱2,941 (no hike found since Feb 2026).
- `baguio.coding` rules unchanged. The scheme is still in force and was suspended on Apr 8–24, Aug 4, Aug 10 and Sep 3, 2026.

**New**
- `route.drive-time` — about 3 h with no traffic (DPWH, 2020).
- `marcos.status` — passable as of Oct 9.
- `baguio.road-closures` — **PMA Road closed** (sinking), plus Baguio–Itogon and Benguet–Nueva Vizcaya (Beckel).
- `baguio.coding-zone` — perimeter roads (Ordinance No. 1 s. 2003).
- `weather.baguio` — PAGASA rainfall normals + record highs/lows by month.
- `emergency.911`, `emergency.nlex` (1-35000, from nlex.com.ph).
