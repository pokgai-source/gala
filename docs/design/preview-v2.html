Road check before you go up

  • Kennon RoadOpen to light vehicles, max 5 tons

    Camp 2 Acrow Bridge is closed, so you take a one-lane detour. Two more one-lane spots at Camp 6. Closed to non-residents in heavy rain.

  • Marcos HighwayPassable

    Take this if you drive a heavy vehicle or it's raining hard.

  • Closed around BaguioNot passable

    PMA Road, PMA Cut-Off Road No. 3, Baguio–Itogon Road at Goldfield, Benguet–Nueva Vizcaya Road at Beckel.
