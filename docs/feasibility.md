# GALA feasibility — summary (2026-10-10)

## Product
Phone-first roadbooks for self-drive trips out of Manila, one link per trip. First: Manila → Baguio.
Next: La Union (San Juan), then Tagaytay/Batangas. Starts as a free hobby tied to Jed's travel vlogging.

## Who pays (later)
- **Not car rental shops** — most don't offer self-drive.
- **Hotels, inns, transient houses** — a roadbook branded with their logo, colors, parking details and contact
  button, sent to guests after booking or at check-in. Small "Made by GALA" footer.
- **Route restaurants and pasalubong shops** — paid **Featured stop** placement, priced against nano-influencer
  posts (about ₱1,000–5,000 per post in PH). Always labelled "Featured".

## Starting test prices (guesses to validate)
- Hotel: ₱1,500–3,000 setup + ₱300–500/month updates.
- Featured stop: ₱500–1,500/month.
- Phase 1: free for 3–5 hotels in exchange for testimonials.

## Roadmap and gates
| Phase | When | Work | Gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Build | Oct–Nov 2026 | Baguio roadbook v1, test with 10 friends | **A:** 10 real trips used it |
| 2. Prove | Dec 2026–Jan 2027 | Free for 3–5 hotels, free featured stops | **B:** 3 hotels send it to guests |
| 3. Charge | Feb–Mar 2027 | Paid hotel test, paid featured stops, move hosting | **C:** 2 paying within 6 weeks, else stop charging or change angle |
| 4. Grow | Apr–Jun 2027 | La Union, Tagaytay, DTI registration, PDF | — |

## Risks and rules that come from them
- **Wrong or old info** on a road trip is worse than none → every fact has a source and checked date; 45-day
  re-check; official sources first.
- **Misleading-ad rules** (Consumer Act) → paid placements labelled "Featured", disclosed in vlogs too.
- **Privacy** → nothing collected; the coding checker runs on the phone.
- **Hosting terms** → GitHub Pages isn't for running a business; move before charging (Gate C).
- **Leaning on other vloggers** until Jed drives the route → credit and link, never copy.
